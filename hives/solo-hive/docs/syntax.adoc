== Solo Syntax (Core Grammar)

This is the Solo dialect core syntax, derived from
`+My grammar (solo).enbf+`.

=== Grammar (EBNF)

....
program          = { top_level };

top_level        = fn_decl
                 | struct_decl
                 | effect_decl
                 | contract_decl
                 | import_decl
                 | comptime_decl
                 | arena_decl;

block            = "{" , { stmt } , "}";

stmt             = expr , ";"
                 | "let" , [ "mut" ] , ident , [ ":" , type ] , "=" , expr , ";"
                 | "if" , expr , block , [ "else" , block ]
                 | "go" , block
                 | [ "return" | "await" ] , expr , ";"
                 | "try" , expr , [ "?" ]
                 | "comptime" , block;

expr             = literal
                 | ident
                 | expr , "(" , [ expr_list ] , ")"
                 | expr , "." , ident
                 | expr , ( "=" | "+" | "-" | "*" | "/" | "==" | "!=" | "<" | ">" | "&&" | "||" ) , expr
                 | "try" , expr
                 | block
                 | "restrict" , expr;

expr_list        = expr , { "," , expr };

type             = "Int"
                 | "String"
                 | "Bool"
                 | ident
                 | type , "->" , type
                 | "Effect" , "<" , type , ">"
                 | [ "&" , [ "mut" ] ] , type
                 | "[" , type , "]"
                 | "{" , { ident , ":" , type } , "}";

fn_decl          = [ "async" | "#[safe]" ] , "fn" , ident , "(" , [ param_list ] , ")"
                 , [ "->" , type ]
                 , [ contract ]
                 , block;

param_list       = ident , ":" , type , { "," , ident , ":" , type };

struct_decl      = "struct" , ident , "{" , { ident , ":" , type } , "}";

effect_decl      = "effect" , ident , "{" , { fn_decl } , "}";

contract         = "where" , { contract_clause };

contract_clause  = "pre:" , expr
                 | "post:" , expr
                 | "invariant:" , expr;

comptime_decl    = "comptime" , block;

arena_decl       = "let" , ident , "=" , "Arena::new()" , ";";

import_decl      = "use" , ident , [ "::" , ident ] , ";";

literal          = int_lit | string_lit | "true" | "false";

int_lit          = digit , { digit };

string_lit       = '"' , { char } , '"';

ident            = letter , { letter | digit | "_" };
....

=== Operator Precedence (lowest -> highest)

[arabic]
. `+||+`
. `+&&+`
. `+==+`, `+!=+`
. `+<+`, `+>+`
. `+++`, `+-+`
. `+*+`, `+/+`
. Unary: `+-+`, `+!+` (if supported in lexer)
. Call `+f(x)+`, field access `+x.y+`

=== Lexical Notes

* Whitespace is ignored except to separate tokens.
* Comments: `+//+` and `+/* */+`.
* Keywords: `+fn+`, `+struct+`, `+effect+`, `+where+`, `+pre+`,
`+post+`, `+invariant+`, `+comptime+`, `+let+`, `+mut+`, `+if+`,
`+else+`, `+go+`, `+return+`, `+await+`, `+try+`, `+restrict+`,
`+true+`, `+false+`.
* Operators: `+= + - * / == != < > && ||+`.

=== Notes

* This spec intentionally excludes Duet/Ensemble constructs.
* Dialect extensions should be layered on top of this core.
